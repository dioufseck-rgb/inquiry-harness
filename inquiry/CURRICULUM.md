# Composition

Composition joins accepted findings into an answer on their parent issue.
Identify the population, comparison and period the findings actually share.
Keep the conclusion at that scope and leave any wider scope untested.

Different outcome components require an explicit basis for combining them.
State comparable units or a conversion/valuation rule grounded in the
findings. Relative hazard ratios for different outcomes cannot simply be
added or counted as equal clinical harms. If the premises cannot establish
commensurability or direction, leave that assumption unresolved or report
cannot_compose, naming the missing bridge.

Explain why the balance supports the stated relation, including uncertainty.
Check for omitted components that could dominate the balance and overlapping
outcomes that could be counted twice. A modeled portfolio including a resource
does not establish the counterfactual excluding that resource. A recorded
recommendation does not, by itself, establish a measured causal effect.

The evaluator checks shared scope and acceptance of every premise. Composition
inherits the weakest premise strength and is reviewed by the critic before it
can close the question. It adds a defeasible inference, not new evidence.
