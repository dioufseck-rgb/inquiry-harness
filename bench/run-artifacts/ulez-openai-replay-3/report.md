# Did the August 2023 expansion of London's Ultra Low Emission Zone reduce roadside NO2 concentrations in outer London?

The evidence gathered does not settle whether the expansion caused lower mean roadside NO2 across newly covered outer London during 29 August 2023–28 August 2024 (C1–C3 remain undecided). It leans toward a modest reduction: the official analysis reports expansion-specific reductions of 0.6 µg/m³ (2.3%) in September 2023, 1.0 µg/m³ (4.3%) in August 2024 and 1.1 µg/m³ (4.8%) in September 2024 relative to its no-expansion counterfactual. These are monthly model-based trend contrasts, not raw before-and-after changes or an established first-year average. Differences in timing and monitoring composition, together with unresolved counterfactual attribution and uncertainty, prevent accepting the requested full-year causal conclusion or magnitude (C1, C15–C19).

## Key judgements

- **low** The full-year causal answer remains undecided, with the evidence leaning toward a reduction because of the official monthly counterfactual estimates, which do not establish the exact-period, area-wide effect required by C1–C3. (C1, C2, C3)  
  rests on: E59 supplies the directional evidence, but its monthly smoothed estimates do not match the annual estimand; E21, E49 and E50 identify attribution and monitoring-comparability limitations.
- **low** The reported reduction grew from 0.6 µg/m³ (2.3%) in September 2023 to 1.0 µg/m³ (4.3%) in August 2024, but those endpoints cannot determine which annual reduction magnitude is correct. (C15, C16, C17)  
  rests on: E59 reports the monthly concentration and percentage contrasts, while E49 documents changing monthly monitor composition rather than the fixed, equally weighted annual panel required by the magnitude claims.
- **low** The independent study's non-significant short-period result does not establish an exactly zero effect over the requested full year. (C3, C19)  
  rests on: E73 reports non-detection over the first three months, and E77 and E83 delimit the study's data to April 2024 and exclude the first two post-implementation weeks from average-effect calculations.
- **moderate** A meaningful policy change took effect on 29 August 2023, extending ULEZ to all London boroughs while retaining the earlier zones and applying emissions standards and a qualified £12.50 daily non-compliance charge. (C20)  
  rests on: Accepted argument A3 rests on the administrative implementation record E111, vehicle-standards table E113, charging qualifications E112 and headline charge E67; these establish the intervention, not its pollution effect.

## What would change this

- A validated expansion-only concentration contrast for 29 August 2023–28 August 2024 showing a reduction, increase or equality against continuation of the pre-existing ULEZ would settle C1–C3; it must hold external determinants comparable while retaining expansion-induced changes.
- Ratified observations and station histories identifying a fixed, comparable outer-London roadside panel, with equally weighted means for the preceding and following twelve months, would settle the descriptive before-and-after alternatives C6–C9.
- An annual expansion-only effect estimate in µg/m³ and percentage terms, with numerical uncertainty bounds and counterfactual sensitivity tests, would distinguish C15–C19 rather than substituting a monthly endpoint for an annual mean.
- Period-specific weather, regional-background and natural-fleet-turnover decompositions would resolve C10–C14 and clarify how much of the observed change belongs to external determinants.
- An authoritative correction to the implementation date or coverage recorded in E111, the standards in E113, or the charging provisions supported by E112 and E67 would change the accepted administrative finding C20.

## Unresolved

- The direction and magnitude of the requested full-year causal effect remain undecided (C1–C3, C15–C19), as does the fixed-panel before-and-after change (C6–C9).
- The direction and size of meteorological, unrelated background-pollution and independent fleet or transport contributions remain unresolved (C10–C14).
- Numerical uncertainty bounds for the expansion-only annual contrast were not retrieved in this inquiry; the all-ULEZ intervals cannot substitute for them.
- Counterfactual validation concerns remain unresolved: treatment–control pre-trends, control-zone spillovers, alternative controls, starting-date sensitivity, concurrent transport policies and preservation of expansion-mediated background changes.
- Site-specific effects, monitor inclusion histories, weighting and representativeness across newly covered outer-London roads remain unresolved.
- The shorter independent study's numerical outer-London traffic-site estimates, uncertainty bounds and a comparable full-year extension were not retrieved in this inquiry.
- Precise M25 boundary treatment and specialist-vehicle provisions were not established by the passages read; these concerns do not overturn the narrower accepted policy-change claim C20.

### A3 — Argument from a record

**Claim.** On 29 August 2023, ULEZ coverage expanded to all London boroughs, adding the outer-London area while retaining the pre-existing central and inner zones. Covered vehicles include cars, vans, minibuses and motorcycles. Vehicles failing the applicable standards must pay £12.50 per day unless covered by a grace period, discount or exemption. Standards include Euro 4 for petrol cars and light vans, Euro 6 for diesel cars and light vans, and Euro 3 for motorcycles.

**Grounds.**
- E1: The report records that ULEZ expanded across all London boroughs on 29 August 2023, with the resulting zone covering 1,500 square kilometres and sharing the heavy-vehicle LEZ boundary; this excerpt does not establish the M25 exclusion, charge amount or emissions standards. — "On 29 August 2023, the Mayor of London expanded the Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) across all London boroughs"
- E2: The report identifies cars, vans, minibuses and motorcycles as vehicle categories subject to ULEZ standards. — "vehicles travelling in the zone that are subject to the ULEZ standards – that is cars, vans, minibuses, and motorcycles."
- E16: The report distinguishes the expanded outer-London area from the pre-existing central and inner zones; its inner-London definition covers the area bounded by the North and South Circular Roads, including central London. — "expanded outer London area (excluding the central and inner London ULEZ area)"
- E19: The report defines the expanded outer-London monitoring area as extending from and including the North and South Circular Roads to the London-wide ULEZ boundary; vehicles detected there may also travel in the pre-existing zones. — "the outer zone (the area from, and including, the North and South Circular Roads, to the London-wide ULEZ boundary based on available cameras)"
- E35: The report defines the expanded outer-London ULEZ area as excluding the existing central and inner zones but including the North and South Circular roads. — "the expanded outer London ULEZ area (excluding the central and inner London area, including the North and South Circular roads)"
- E67: The article reports a usual daily charge of £12.50 for older, more-polluting vehicles driven within the zone, with exceptions. — "The charge for more-polluting older vehicles is usually £12.50 a day to drive within the zone, with some exceptions."
- E111: ULEZ expanded from its existing central and inner London coverage to all London boroughs on 29 August 2023; the resulting zone covered 1,500 square kilometres and nine million people. — "On the 29 August 2023, to bring the health benefits of cleaner air to even more people the Mayor further expanded the ULEZ to cover all London boroughs."
- E112: Vehicles failing the applicable emissions standards must pay a charge unless covered by a grace period, discount or exemption. This passage does not specify the daily charge amount. — "Vehicles that do not meet certain emissions standards, and are not otherwise subject to a grace period, discount, or exemption, must pay a charge to travel within the city."
- E113: ULEZ standards are Euro 4 for petrol cars and smaller vans, Euro 6 for diesel cars and smaller vans, and Euro 3 for motorcycles. The table also applies the Euro 4 petrol and Euro 6 diesel standards to larger vans up to 3.5 tonnes and minibuses up to 5 tonnes. — "Cars Euro 4 (Petrol) Euro 6 (Diesel) ULEZ Motorbikes Euro 3 ULEZ Smaller vans Euro 4 (Petrol) Euro 6 (Diesel) ULEZ"
